Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you have to manage.

Nova works in the background so your only job is the thing in front of you.

Intelligent day planning

Nova maps your tasks against your real energy curve and proposes one priority per block, so you always know what the next 90 minutes are for.

Attention guard

During a session, Nova holds the line. Non-urgent interruptions are batched and delivered at the end of the block — not the moment you finally got started.

Calm, honest insights

No scores, no badges, no guilt. Just a quiet read on where your attention actually went, and one gentle suggestion for tomorrow.
